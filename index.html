02 / About

Built by operators who have defended these networks.

Forge Solutions is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business founded by a Marine Corps veteran with twenty-five years of experience in defense, intelligence, and cybersecurity.

Our founder has served at the Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) for over fifteen years — as both a military officer and a contractor — with hands-on architecture and engineering experience across boundary defense systems, defensive cyber operations analytics, and the modernization programs behind DoD enterprise security.

What we bring is the combination of senior technical depth and the discipline to lead through complex, high-stakes missions. We have planned operations in combat, modernized cyber defense systems at enterprise scale, and consistently brought people together — across government, industry, and research — to accomplish what matters.
